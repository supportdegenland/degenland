Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026. This policy explains how DegenLand collects and uses your personal data.

1. Who we are

Your personal data is processed by the team that runs DegenLand ("we"). You can reach us through the official channels listed in section 14.

2. Data we collect

Account data: email address, nickname, and your Telegram ID and username if you link Telegram.

Game data: your farm, miners, balances, purchases, marketplace activity and referrals.

Withdrawal data: Solana addresses you save and your request history.

Technical data: IP address, device and browser information, timestamps and language.

Communications: messages you send to support.

3. Why we use it

To provide the game and your account, and to process deposits and withdrawals (performance of the contract).

To prevent fraud, multiple accounts, bots and abuse, and to keep the game secure (legitimate interest).

To comply with legal obligations, including anti-money-laundering checks where required.

To send notifications you have enabled through Telegram or email.

4. Advertising

The game shows video ads from third-party ad networks. These networks may collect data such as advertising identifiers, IP address and device information under their own privacy policies.

5. Who we share data with

With service providers that help us run the game (hosting, email delivery, Telegram, ad networks, analytics), under agreements that protect your data, and with authorities when the law requires it. We do not sell personal data.

6. Blockchain

Transactions on Solana are public and irreversible. We cannot erase data recorded on a public blockchain.

7. International transfers

Your data may be processed outside your country by the service providers we use (hosting, email delivery), under their own data protection safeguards.

8. Retention

We keep your data while your account is active and afterwards for as long as needed for legal, security and dispute purposes, for example transaction records, for the period required by law.

9. Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability of your data, object to processing and withdraw consent at any time by writing to us on Discord. You may also complain to your data protection authority. Some rights may be limited where we must keep data for legal obligations or fraud prevention.

10. Security

We use encryption in transit, one-time login codes, access controls and two-factor authentication for our staff. No system is completely secure: please keep your credentials safe.

11. Minors

DegenLand is for adults only (18+). If we find that a minor has registered, we will delete the account.

12. Local storage and cookies

We use your browser's local storage to remember your language and your game session. We do not use tracking cookies ourselves; ad networks may set their own.

13. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced in the game or by email/Telegram.

14. Contact

Support: Discord. News and announcements: Telegram.
